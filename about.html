About The Sweet Bee

Hi, I'm Brooklyn!

Welcome to The Sweet Bee—I’m so happy you’re here!

I’m the baker and cookie artist behind every order. Baking has always held a special place in my heart. Growing up, some of my favorite memories were spent in the kitchen with my mom, measuring ingredients, making a mess, and creating something delicious together.

I’ve also always been drawn to art and creativity, so it’s no surprise that those two passions eventually found their way together.

Where It All Began

The Sweet Bee began in the most unexpected way. One day, my daughter came across a video of someone decorating sugar cookies and asked if we could try it.

We started making cookies together just for fun, and before long, what began as a simple mother-daughter activity grew into a business I never imagined I’d have. It’s been an incredible journey, and I’m so thankful for where it has led.

Family at the Heart of It

I married my high school sweetheart in 2020, and together we’re raising our three wonderful children—a curious and creative daughter and two adventurous little boys.

One of the greatest blessings of owning The Sweet Bee is that I get to build a business I truly love while still being a full-time mom. My family is at the heart of everything I create, and I pour that same love and attention into every cookie that leaves my kitchen.

Made for Life's Sweetest Moments

I grew up in Idaho Falls, graduated from Bonneville High School, and am one of four siblings. Community, family, and celebrating life’s special moments have always been important to me.

Whether you’re celebrating a birthday, welcoming a new baby, getting married, or simply making an ordinary day a little sweeter, it’s an honor to create something unique just for you.
